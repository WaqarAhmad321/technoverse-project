## Judging criteria

Replace these with the organisers' own if they give any.

1. **Problem and impact.** A real person with a real pain, and a solution that would change their day.
2. **Innovation.** Something a judge has not seen three times already today.
3. **Technical execution.** It works, live, with real data moving through it.
4. **Design and experience.** It looks finished and a stranger can use it without help.
5. **Presentation.** A clear story, a demo that lands, honest answers.
